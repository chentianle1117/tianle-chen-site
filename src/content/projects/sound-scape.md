## How can parametric acoustic simulation help design a caring space for the sensorially impaired?

Sound Scape is a care facility in the Paris 13th arrondissement designed for visually and hearing-impaired individuals. Acoustic simulations drive the geometry of each room, treating sound behavior as a generator of form. The project challenges the convention of inclusive design as retrofit — disability becomes a primary design driver that benefits everyone in the building.
