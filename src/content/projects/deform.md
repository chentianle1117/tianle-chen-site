Deform is a formal exercise in continuous deformation — a building envelope developed through a controlled sequence of geometric transformations. The project asks how far a single surface can be pushed before it stops reading as one form, and uses that limit as a tool for absorbing programmatic and structural demands without breaking the envelope's continuity.
